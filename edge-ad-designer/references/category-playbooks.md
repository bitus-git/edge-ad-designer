# Category playbooks

## Tobacco, nicotine and vaping policy (harm reduction, flavour bans, taxes)

**Core move:** argue about **policy, rights, science, prices and consequences**, never about the product.

**Never show:**
- devices, pods, packs or pouch tins
- vapour clouds, or anyone inhaling or exhaling
- brand names or logos of tobacco or vape companies
- flavour cues such as fruit or candy (these read as youth appeal and get flagged fast)
- anything that makes using the product look attractive

**Show instead:**
- typography, and stat cards with sources
- price tags and receipts, tax stamps
- red tape, rubber stamps and permit forms (use a clearly fictional "Department of…" name)
- a gavel, a capitol dome, a ballot (skip the dome and any official seals in Taboola thumbnails, where government symbols are restricted)
- an empty shop shelf with no products visible, a "CLOSED" sign
- real adults as faces (testimony, not consumption)
- a nanny or busybody as an illustrated archetype

**Claims:**
- Don't say "vaping is safe", "helps you quit" or "95% safer". Unapproved modified-risk and cessation claims are an FDA problem in the US, and platforms reject them as unsubstantiated.
- Say instead: "Adults deserve accurate information about less harmful alternatives", or "Far less harmful than smoking — [named public health body, year]", with the citation on the image and the full source on the landing page.
- Prefer sources the audience sees as neutral or adversarial (government health agencies, journals) over your own research.

**Targeting:** 21+ (25+ talent where alcohol-style rules apply), the issue category declared, no EU, no TikTok or LinkedIn paid.

**Landing page:** reviewers check it too. Use sourced claims, a petition or rep-contact form, and **no links to retailers**.

**Proven frames for this category:**
- **Liberty / nanny state:** "Adults can make adult choices." (Archetype: the Center for Consumer Freedom's "Nanny Bloomberg" 2012. Today, use illustration rather than a doctored photo of a real official.)
- **Harm reduction (care/harm, works for the centre-left):** "Bans push people back to cigarettes."
- **Regressive tax (fairness):** "This tax hits the lowest-paid hardest."
- **Prohibition backfires:** black markets, unregulated products, policing.
- **Identity and voting bloc:** "We Vape, We Vote" (2019–22) turned users into a constituency. Use identity + a named decision-maker + a consequence ("we remember in November").
- **Truth-campaign inversion:** the rebellious, non-preachy tone, pointed at "who's really manipulating you": bureaucrats, cronyism, or pharma lobbying, as appropriate.

## Political and issue advocacy (general)

**Required on every ad:**
- the issue category declared and the advertiser authorised (Meta SIEP; Google election verification if it names an officeholder, candidate, party or ballot measure; X certification; Snap form)
- a verified "Paid for by"
- no EU targeting
- election blackout windows checked

**Allowed and effective:**
- naming officials and their **official acts** with citations
- "Tell [Rep] to vote NO on [Bill] before [real date]"
- satire of institutions
- myth/fact
- price and loss framing
- real constituent testimony

**Never:**
- discourage voting, question an election's legitimacy, or claim victory early
- fabricate quotes, use doctored photorealistic images of officials, or use undisclosed AI
- attack someone's personal life
- target by protected characteristics

**Mobilisation vs. persuasion:**
- Warm audiences get one imperative action with a real deadline.
- Cold audiences get a soft or absent CTA ("See the numbers"), with retargeting used to mobilise later.
- For contact-your-rep campaigns, ask people to add a personal line on the landing page. Staffers discount identical form emails.

## Alcohol policy (e.g. minimum unit pricing, sin taxes, delivery bans)

- **Advocacy** follows the issue-ad rules.
- **If the ad is commercial alcohol:**
  - target the local drinking age (21+ in the US)
  - talent must look 25+ on Snap and X
  - no health, social or sexual success claims
  - no rapid or excessive drinking
  - add a responsible-drinking line where required
- **Visual:** price comparisons, receipts, a pub's chalkboard, people socialising with no drinking emphasis.

## Cannabis and CBD

- THC is effectively unadvertisable on every platform.
- CBD is topical-only with LegitScript certification on Meta and Google, and banned on TikTok.
- **Policy advocacy** (legalisation, banking access, decriminalisation) runs as an issue ad. Show no product, no consumption and no leaf-as-hero imagery. Use justice, tax revenue and safety frames.

## Gambling policy

- **Advocacy** (opposing ad bans, stake limits or affordability checks) is an issue ad. Show no betting interface, odds, or "win" imagery.
- **Frames that work:** adult autonomy, privacy (affordability checks mean bank-statement surveillance), black-market displacement.
