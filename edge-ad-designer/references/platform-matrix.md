# Platform matrix: where advocacy and regulated-topic ads can run

Researched 2026-10-27. *[verify]* = could not confirm against a primary source; check before relying on it.

## Restricted vs. prohibited

- **Prohibited**: there is no path to approval. Examples: vape promotion everywhere; policy advocacy on TikTok and LinkedIn.
- **Restricted**: allowed once you pass the gates (authorisation, certification, Special Ad Category, age targeting, country lists). Examples: social-issue ads on Meta; election ads on Google; alcohol everywhere.

Put every ad in one of these two tiers before writing any copy. The restricted tier is where the edge is.

## Time-sensitive

- **Meta US midterm blackout: Oct 27 to Nov 3, 2026.** No new social-issue, electoral or political ads can start in the US. Ads that served before Oct 27 keep running, but only budget, bid, end date and pause can be changed. Expect Meta to repeat this before future US general elections.
- **Google** paused US election ads after polls closed in 2020 and 2024. *[verify]* whether it will do so in 2026.

## Region: the EU is effectively closed to policy ads

The EU Transparency and Targeting of Political Advertising regulation (TTPA, Reg. 2024/900) has applied since Oct 10, 2025. It covers ads "liable and designed to influence… a legislative or regulatory process" at EU, national, regional or local level. An ad opposing a member-state vape tax counts, no matter who runs it.

Platforms that stopped serving these ads in the EU:
- Meta (from Oct 6, 2025)
- Google and YouTube (from Sept 2025)
- LinkedIn
- TikTok (already banned them)
- Snap (from Oct 1, 2025; neutral social-issue ads are reviewed case by case)

X and Reddit EU status: *[verify]*.

The UK is not in the EU. Meta and Google still accept UK issue ads with authorisation, and UK Elections Act digital imprints apply.

## Political and issue ads by platform

| Platform | Issue advocacy (e.g. oppose a flavour ban) | Gates | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta (FB/IG)** | ✅ Allowed outside the EU as a SIEP ad ("Social Issues, Elections or Politics") | Advertiser authorisation (ID plus a local address), a verified "Paid for by" disclaimer, the Special Ad Category declared at campaign level, targeting only in authorised countries | US issue topics include Economy, Health, and Political values & governance. A vape ad is SIEP even with no candidate named. AI disclosure is required for photorealistic altered or synthetic media. Meta's own gen-AI ad tools are not available for SIEP ads. Ads stay in the Ad Library for 7 years. Washington State bans state and local ads. |
| **Google / YouTube** | ✅ Allowed. If the ad names no officeholder, candidate, party or ballot measure, it is an ordinary ad in the US | Naming one makes it an election ad: verification, an automatic "Paid for by", and targeting limited to geography, age, gender and context | Restricted in some US states (ID, MD, NV, NJ, WA) and in several countries. Keywords like "vape", "e-cigarette" or "smoking" often trigger disapproval, so plan to appeal. Realistic altered content in election ads must be marked as altered or synthetic. |
| **TikTok** | ❌ Banned everywhere | — | The ban covers paid ads, branded creator content and Promote. It explicitly includes "policy proposals" and legislative outcomes. NGOs can run only limited non-political ads through a sales rep. |
| **X** | ✅ Allowed in permitted countries (incl. US and UK) | Political campaigning (asking for votes or money) needs certification. Cause-based ads need cause certification, geo targeting no finer than state, and no political keyword targeting | *[verify]*: X policy pages returned 403. Reported Oct 2026 plan to bar "ugly, shrill" ads. |
| **LinkedIn** | ❌ Banned globally, including ads for or against any "law, regulation" | — | Promoting thought leadership or research ("New report on…") can run. An ad telling people to oppose a bill cannot. Paid political endorsements by creators are also banned. |
| **Snapchat** | ✅ Allowed outside the EU and Canada | Political advertiser form, human review, "Paid for by", Political Ads Library | Snap may ask for proof of factual claims. Attack ads are allowed, but not attacks on someone's personal life. No manipulated likenesses. |
| **Reddit** | ⚠️ US federal-level only (historical policy) | Manual review, "Paid for by" inside the creative, comments left open | *[verify]* the 2025–26 scope. Assume state-level issue ads are not allowed. AI content must be labelled. Attack ads were prohibited as of 2024. |

### Quick answer: "Can we run an ad against a vape tax or flavour ban?"

- **Meta**: yes, as a SIEP ad, outside the EU and outside blackout windows.
- **Google**: yes, and it is an ordinary ad if it names no officeholder. Expect keyword disapprovals.
- **X**: yes, with certification if needed.
- **Snap**: yes, outside the EU and Canada.
- **Reddit**: maybe, federal only.
- **TikTok**: no.
- **LinkedIn**: no.
- **Taboola**: maybe, after account-manager pre-clearance.
- **Teads/Outbrain**: US only, with no attacks on candidates.
- **Yahoo**: after pre-approval.
- **Google Display**: yes, and you'll lose inventory to publishers who block "Politics".

## Alcohol, cannabis and CBD, gambling (brief)

- **Alcohol** is restricted everywhere: target the local drinking age (21+ in the US), make no health claims, and avoid blocked countries. Snap requires talent to look 25+ plus a responsible-drinking message. X requires 25+ talent. LinkedIn allows it only through sales in some countries.
- **Cannabis (THC)** is effectively banned on every platform.
- **CBD and hemp**:
  - Meta: topical only, with LegitScript certification and written permission, 18+, no treat or cure claims.
  - Google: topical only, in some US states, with LegitScript certification.
  - TikTok: banned.
- **Gambling** needs written permission or certification and a licence per country, with 18+ targeting (or the local age). LinkedIn bans gambling entirely.
- **Advocacy about these topics** (for example opposing an alcohol minimum unit price) is an issue ad and follows the political rules above, not the product rules.

## Account risk

- **Meta**: running issue content without the declared category or without authorisation leads to disapproval, then restrictions on the ad account or Page. Tobacco violations count against account integrity. The most common mistake is running issue ads from a personal ad account that isn't authorised.
- **Google**: most violations get a 7-day warning before suspension. **Egregious** violations mean immediate suspension with no way back. These include circumventing the system, misrepresentation, and false association with a public figure (since Mar 2024).
- **TikTok and LinkedIn**: repeatedly submitting political ads flags the account.
